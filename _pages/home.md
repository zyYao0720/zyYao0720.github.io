My research focuses on the application of artificial intelligence in environmental science, with an emphasis on integrating data-driven methods with process-based understanding of atmospheric environments. I am currently working on global air quality forecasting, aiming to improve the predictive accuracy and spatiotemporal resolution of fine particulate matter, ozone, and other key atmospheric pollutants at the global scale using machine learning, deep learning, and multisource environmental data assimilation. This work supports air pollution risk assessment, environmental health impact analysis, and atmospheric environmental management, while contributing to the development of efficient, reliable, and interpretable intelligent forecasting methods.

### About me

I am currently a master's student at the School of Data Science and Engineering, East China Normal University, and expect to graduate in June 2027. I received my B.Eng. degree in Data Science and Big Data Technology from the College of Information Technology, Shanghai Ocean University, where I studied from 2020 to 2024. During my undergraduate studies, I conducted research under the supervision of [Associate Professor Zhaocai Wang](https://orcid.org/0000-0003-1396-6835), focusing on the application of artificial intelligence in hydrology. I have published five SCI-indexed papers as the first author, including two ESI Highly Cited Papers. I am currently conducting interdisciplinary research at the intersection of atmospheric science and artificial intelligence under the supervision of [Assistant Professor Jing Wei](https://weijing-rs.github.io/index.html) at the College of Environmental Sciences and Engineering, Peking University, with a particular focus on global air quality forecasting and related intelligent modeling methods.
